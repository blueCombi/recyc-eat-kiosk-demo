Kiosk Settings

Configure points, redemption rules, rewards, and kiosk operation.

Points per item

The bin's inductive sensor decides plastic or can, and the three IR beams decide the size. Each combination gets its own award below.

Redemption rules

Reward picker unlock

FINISH offers the 4-coil picker once the shopper reaches this many points, or the cheapest coil cost — whichever is lower. Each coil still has its own price below.

Default: 50. Spaghetti is 40, so 40 already unlocks the picker.

Reward point costs

Coils 1–4 match the kiosk buttons and the vending motors. Names and costs here are what shoppers see on Redeem.

Kiosk operations

Kiosk open for use

When off, the public kiosk shows a temporarily unavailable message.

Auto-print receipts

Print a QR receipt automatically after a successful session.

Needs the local print server running on port 4000.

Idle session timeout (seconds)

Return to the home screen after this many seconds of inactivity.

Low-stock alert threshold

Flag inventory items when remaining quantity falls to this number or below.
