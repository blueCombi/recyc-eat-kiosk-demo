Connecting to local scanner service.

Do this in order

  1. Plug the scanner USB into the PC. If it has a round DC jack, also plug a 5V power adapter there (leave RS232 unplugged).
  2. Aim at each QR below and trigger a scan (wait for beep). Config codes are accepted even when Notepad stays blank.
  3. Unplug USB for 5 seconds, plug back in.
  4. Open Notepad and scan a product barcode or receipt QR. Text should appear — or this page’s status turns green.
  5. Then open Scan QR Receipt.
